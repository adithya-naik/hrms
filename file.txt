Who gets what (and what’s stored)

On Apply (createLeave)

Emails:

Manager → “New Leave Request” (includes type, dates, reason).

HR → “New Leave Request” (includes type, dates, reason).

Requester → “We received your request” (includes type, dates, reason).

Notifications table:

Manager, HR → “New Leave Request …”

Requester → “Leave Submitted …”

AuditLog: CREATE with newValues = leave.

On Approve (approveLeave)

Email: Requester → “Leave Approved”.

Notification: Requester → “Leave Approved …”

AuditLog: APPROVE with status change.

On Reject (rejectLeave)

Email: Requester → “Leave Rejected” (includes rejection reason).

Notification: Requester → “Leave Rejected …”

AuditLog: REJECT with status + reason.

On Cancel (cancelLeave)

Notification: Requester → “Leave Cancelled …”

AuditLog: CANCEL.

Email logs: every send attempt is logged like

✅ Email sent | leaveSubmitted | to=user@company.com

❌ Email failed | leaveRequest | to=manager@company.com | err=...
